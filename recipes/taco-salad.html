Taco Salad

A very simple recipe, you can customize it in so many ways. I make this in bulk and eat it throughout the week, it is addicting!

Here is what you are going to need:

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1/2 lb shredded cheese
  • 2 Large romaine lettuce chopped
  • 2 large tomatoes chopped
  • 1 can black olives
  • 1 bottle of salsa, i use the chunky Pace medium salsa
  • 1 bottle of thousand island dressing
  • 1 bag of tortilla chips

Instructions:

  1. Cook the ground beef, drain and season. I use Mateo's taco seasoning from Costco.
  2. While meat is cooking prep all the remaining ingredients.
  3. Do not add the meat until all is prepped because it melts the cheese and wilts the lettuce.
  4. Mix everything together and serve.

A good meal if you are on a bulk since there are a lot of calories in dressing. you can use a low calorie dressing if you are watching your calorie intake and switch out the beef with ground chicken or turkey.

Top the mix with sour cream, avacado, black beans, corn, cilantro. I eat mine with no extra toppings, straight out of the mixing bowl.

Provecho!
