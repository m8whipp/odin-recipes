Clam Chowder

Recipe from a restaurant my parents went to on their honeymoon in Morro Bay. The town is famous for having a really big rock. I have a picture of it.

Ingredients:

  • 4 strips bacon
  • 1 large chopped onion
  • 4 medium sized potatoes
  • 1 can cream style corn
  • 1 quart milk
  • 2 cups clams
  • salt and pepper to taste

Instructions:

  1. Cut bacon into small peices and fry with onion, drain fat
  2. Add potatoes, cut into 1/4 in cubes and add 2 cups of water
  3. Cook until tender, then add milk, salt and pepper, and corn
  4. Finely chopped clams should be added last.
